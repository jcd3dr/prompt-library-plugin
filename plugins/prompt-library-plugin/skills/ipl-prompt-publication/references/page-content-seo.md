# Spanish educational page and SEO

Write for anyone who wants good results from image or video generators without knowing how to write prompts: someone who wants photos of themselves in different styles, a person selling a car, a small seller, a creator, a marketer. Assume no prior knowledge; plain everyday Spanish, technical terms explained in one phrase the first time. Title, summary, `mode_help`, education, field labels, `prompt_es`, alt text, result labels and SEO are in natural Spanish. The executable prompt and replacement examples are in English.

The page already shows numbered usage steps generated from the mode, so `mode_help` says only what the reader must supply (which photo to upload) and what the mode does. Education explains (a) what practical task the template solves, (b) who uses it, (c) for img2image, what to upload and how the fictional example illustrates it, (d) which prompt fragments control what, (e) how to adapt each customizable part, (f) what to check before commercial use, (g) known model limitations, and (h) variations, without promises the results do not show.

`prompt_es` is a faithful, readable translation of the full prompt so the reader understands what it asks for; it is never a second prompt to copy. `difficulty`: `basico` (copy and paste), `intermedio` (needs choices or an input photo), `avanzado` (video, several steps or precise control).

Metadata: 1–2 sentence summary, one or more focused existing categories (primary first), 3–6 tags, `meta_title` ≤ 60 characters, `meta_description` ≤ 160 characters, free access unless specified, deliberate `noindex`. Do not call a fictional specimen a real customer product or imply trademark rights.
